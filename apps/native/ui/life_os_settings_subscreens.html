Planning style
How the AI distributes priorities and habits across your week.
Light
2–3 priorities per day. Habits only. Space for rest and the unexpected.
Best for recovery weeks, low energy periods, or when life is unpredictable.
Balanced
3–4 priorities per day. Full habit stack. One deep work block daily.
Your current setting. Sustainable pace with consistent progress.
Intensive
4–5 priorities per day. Multiple deep work blocks. Stretch goals included.
Best for sprint weeks when you're energised and have a clear goal.
This also controls how the AI responds to your energy check-ins — low energy scores will nudge the AI toward a lighter day even on Intensive.

AI tone
How Claude communicates across suggestions, nudges, summaries, and the weekly review.
Direct
Short, clear, action-oriented. No softening.
"Finance review is 4 days overdue. Do it today."
Coaching
Warmer framing. Context before action. Better for wellness and faith domains.
"The finance review has been waiting — finishing it today would close the week cleanly."
Minimal
Numbers and facts only. No interpretive language.
"Finance: review due. 4 days elapsed."
Applies to: Today suggestions · domain nudges · plan summaries · weekly review language · approval descriptions · cross-domain observations.
